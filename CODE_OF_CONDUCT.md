# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in `qqbot-toolkit` to be a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background,
or preferred tools.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy: never publish another person's credentials, OpenIDs, chat
  content, or other personal data.
- Follow the security disclosure process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of
  posting vulnerabilities publicly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior, spam, or bad-faith security reports.
- Encouraging or assisting abuse of the QQ platform, its users, or third parties.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.

Report concerns privately to **MioRay@foxmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed in
good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.

This is a short, project-specific code of conduct; it is not a substitute for
applicable platform rules or law.
